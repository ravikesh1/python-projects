When to fly

Cheapest months

February, March, and November typically have the lowest fares — Fiji's wet season.

Peak season

June through September is dry season; expect 30–50% higher fares and book 3–4 months out.

Best day to book

Tuesday and Wednesday departures average 12% cheaper than weekend flights.

Visa & entry

Most visitors get a free 4-month visitor permit on arrival. Passport must be valid 6+ months.
